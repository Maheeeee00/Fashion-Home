About Us

A Woman-Owned Company16+ Years ExperienceSince 2010

One complete sourcing solution

Fashion Home Textile + Apparels is a woman-owned home textile and apparel manufacturing company, led by CEO Aysha Bukhari. Since 2010 we have served retail clients across North America with 16+ years of experience.

We work directly for you — no vendor middle man. Design, sampling, sourcing, packaging, production, shipping and ESG compliance: we do it all, through our network of vendors across Pakistan, India and China.
